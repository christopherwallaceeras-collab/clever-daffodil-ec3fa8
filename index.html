What is Vela?

Vela Athletics is a mobile fitness app that delivers hyper-personalized training and nutrition guidance. Whether you train with our AI coach or connect with a real personal trainer through the app, Vela builds your program around your goals, your experience level, and your progress.

  • Personalized workout programs Training plans tailored to your goals, equipment, and schedule — with in-app set-by-set logging, rest timers, and progress history.
  • Real trainers, on your phone Connect with a certified personal trainer for custom programming, video form feedback, goal setting, and scheduled check-ins.
  • Nutrition tracking Log meals, track calories and macros against your goals, and monitor water intake — all in one place.
  • Progress that stays with you Weight and body composition trends, workout streaks, personal records, and daily health stats.
